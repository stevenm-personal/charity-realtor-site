# Image rights record

This is an internal maintenance record for photography and other image assets used by the Charity Menefee website. Images photographed or provided by Steven or Charity are treated as owner-supplied and cleared for use on this site. Third-party images should have their source and permission status documented here.

An unresolved third-party image may temporarily retain a source hint in its filename as a breadcrumb. Once its rights are resolved, the source and permission details should live in this record rather than being required in the public asset filename.

## Owner-supplied photography

Images photographed or provided by Steven or Charity for this website normally don't require an individual rights entry. If an owner-supplied image later has special restrictions, document it separately here.

## Third-party image workflow

1. Keep a source breadcrumb in the asset filename while permission is unresolved.
2. Obtain permission from the image owner or an authorized source.
3. Save the permission evidence under `docs/image-rights/`.
4. Record the permission and the specific permitted uses in this file.
5. Once cleared, optionally rename the website asset with a clean, descriptive filename.
6. Update the record if permission is later expanded.

Third-party images used with permission should normally receive the documented courtesy attribution at each public use unless a specific permission or design decision says otherwise.

Unresolved source hints should remain in place until the associated rights are resolved. Permanent asset names should use lowercase kebab-case, describe the visible subject, and avoid labels such as `final`, `new`, or `v2`. Rights and license details belong in this record rather than in permanent filenames.
